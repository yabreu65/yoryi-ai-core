# Tickets FAQ

## How do I report an issue?

1. Go to the Tickets module
2. Click "New Ticket"
3. Fill in:
   * Title (brief description)
   * Description (what happened, when, where)
   * Category (plumbing, electrical, common area, etc.)
   * Priority (low/medium/high/urgent)
4. Submit

---

## What information should I include?

Good ticket includes:

* What happened (clear description)
* When it happened (date/time)
* Where (specific unit area if applicable)
* Photos if possible

Avoid:
* Vague titles like "Problem" or "Issue"
* Missing location details

---

## How long does it take to resolve a ticket?

Resolution time depends on priority:

* **urgent** — same day or next business day
* **high** — 1-3 business days
* **medium** — 3-7 business days
* **low** — up to 14 business days

---

## Can I follow up on my ticket?

Yes.

1. Go to Tickets module
2. Find your ticket
3. Add a comment

Or ask the assistant: "What's the status of my ticket?"

---

## Who can see my ticket?

* **You** — always see your own tickets
* **Your building operator/admin** — see all tenant tickets
* **Internal comments** — only visible to operators/admins

---

## How do I know if my ticket was resolved?

When status changes to "resolved":

* You receive a notification
* The ticket shows in your inbox
* You can confirm it's fixed or request follow-up

If no response in 7 days, ticket auto-closes.

---

## Can I create a ticket for another unit?

Only administrators can create tickets for any unit.

Residents can only create tickets for their assigned unit.

---

## What categories are available?

Common categories:

* plumbing
* electrical
* common areas
* noise
* cleaning
* security
* elevators
* other

---

## Can I cancel my ticket?

You can request cancellation by adding a comment.

Operators can cancel tickets if:

* duplicate
* resolved another way
* invalid
